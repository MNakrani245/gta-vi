GTA 6 official trailers

Watch Trailer 1 and Trailer 2 from Rockstar Games, then explore what the official footage confirms about Jason, Lucia, Vice City and Leonida.

Updated July 11, 2026
Quick guide

Rockstar Games has released two numbered GTA VI trailers. Trailer 1 introduced the modern Vice City setting and Lucia; Trailer 2 expanded the story around Jason, Lucia and the wider state of Leonida.

Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2

Trailer 2 focuses on the relationship between Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos and gives a broader look at robberies, nightlife, vehicles, side characters and locations across Leonida.

Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1

Trailer 1 established the tone: beaches, nightlife, social media clips, crime and the contrast between Leonida’s bright scenery and dangerous undercurrent.

What the trailers officially confirm

  • Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the central protagonists.
  • The story spans Vice City and the wider state of Leonida.
  • The announced platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • The current release date is November 19, 2026.
