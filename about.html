Policy updated July 11, 2026
Identity

GTA 6 Countdown is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Our goal is to make confirmed information easy to find, verify and share.

Editorial standards

Primary sources first

Release dates, platforms, characters and locations are checked against Rockstar Games and Take-Two materials whenever possible. Secondary reporting is used only when it adds context and is clearly attributed.

Confirmed information is labelled

We separate official facts from predictions, rumours and fan reconstructions. Leaked pre-release footage is not published or promoted.

Corrections and freshness

Pages include a visible reviewed or updated date. When an official announcement changes a fact, the relevant page and structured data should be updated together.

Original value

Our long-term focus is original PS5 gameplay, commentary, guides and visual coverage—not copied clips or scraped articles.

Official social channels

Our YouTube channel is @gtavi-ps5. Our Instagram account is @gta6countdownlive. Links elsewhere should be treated cautiously unless they point back to these profiles.

Trademark notice

Grand Theft Auto, GTA, GTA VI and related names, artwork and imagery belong to Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. Their presence here identifies the subject of this independent fan resource.
