GTA 6 characters

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos lead a confirmed cast of criminals, entrepreneurs, artists and outsiders spread across Leonida.

Source-checked July 11, 2026
Who are the GTA 6 protagonists?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the two central protagonists. Their partnership sits at the heart of a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida.

Jason Duval

Jason grew up around criminals, served in the Army and later worked for drug runners in the Leonida Keys. Meeting Lucia may change the direction of his life.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia learned to fight young and landed in Leonida Penitentiary after fighting for her family. Fresh out, she is determined to make smarter moves and build a better life.

Confirmed supporting characters

Cal Hampton

Jason’s friend and a fellow associate of Brian, Cal is happiest at home monitoring communications and following conspiracy theories.

Boobie Ike

A Vice City legend whose business interests span real estate, nightlife and music, with a major stake in Only Raw Records.

Dre'Quan Priest

An aspiring music mogul working with Boobie Ike to turn Only Raw Records into a serious force.

Real Dimez

Bae-Luxe and Roxy are longtime friends who transformed street hustle and social-media visibility into a rap career.

Raul Bautista

An experienced criminal operator connected to high-risk scores and the people willing to attempt them.

Brian Heder

A veteran Keys smuggler who lets Jason stay at one of his properties in return for help with local work.

What is confirmed—and what is not

This guide uses names and background details published by Rockstar Games. Casting speculation, unverified leaks and fan theories are intentionally excluded. That makes the page easier for readers and retrieval systems to cite accurately.
