GTA 6 map and locations

Grand Theft Auto VI returns to Vice City and expands across Leonida, a fictional sunshine state of islands, wetlands, ports, industry and wilderness.

Source-checked July 11, 2026
Direct answer

GTA 6 is set in Leonida. Rockstar has confirmed Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga as featured destinations.

Vice City

Leonida’s neon capital combines beaches, luxury towers, nightlife, dense streets and a criminal economy beneath the sunshine.

Leonida Keys

A tropical island chain of turquoise water, bridges, boats and communities shaped by the sea.

Grassrivers

Wide wetlands and mangrove country where airboats, wildlife and isolated routes replace city streets.

Port Gellhorn

A rougher coastal destination associated with roadside motels, faded attractions and lives beyond Vice City’s polished image.

Ambrosia

Leonida’s industrial interior, defined by heavy infrastructure, work and the communities surrounding it.

Mount Kalaga

A northern wilderness region of rocky roads, forests and open country far from the neon coast.

Is the complete GTA 6 map available?

No complete official map has been published. Fan maps can be interesting, but they remain reconstructions. We label only Rockstar-confirmed places as confirmed and do not present speculative borders or scale as fact.

How large is Leonida?

Rockstar describes GTA VI as its biggest and most immersive evolution of the series, but an official numerical map size has not been published. Comparisons based only on trailer angles should be treated as estimates.
