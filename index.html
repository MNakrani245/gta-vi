Need to know

GTA 6
FAQ.

When is GTA 6 coming out?

Rockstar Games currently lists Grand Theft Auto VI for November 19, 2026. This countdown runs to midnight on that date in your local timezone.

Which platforms are confirmed?

PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S are the announced launch platforms. Rockstar Games has not announced a PC release date on the official GTA VI page.

Where is GTA 6 set?

The game takes place across Leonida, a fictional state inspired by Florida. Confirmed areas include Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.

Who are the main characters?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the central protagonists. After an easy score goes wrong, the pair become caught in a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida.

Is this an official Rockstar website?

No. GTA 6 Countdown is an independent fan-made countdown and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
